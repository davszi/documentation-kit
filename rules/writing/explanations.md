# Explanations and reading layers

## Story and reading layers

Order by understanding: purpose, prerequisites, ingredients, mechanism, examples, evidence and limits. Introduce the instrument before its numbers and evidence before a shipping consequence. Define terms before using them. Explain each concept or formula in one canonical place and link to it elsewhere. Keep a brief result label or scope condition beside the evidence whenever it affects how the reader should interpret that evidence. Link to the owning record for the full scope and rationale. An essential repeated equation needs a back-reference.

Keep one visible Story. Optional depth uses typed folds:

```markdown
<!-- fold: math Derivation of the bound -->
Supporting derivation.
<!-- /fold -->
```

Use `math`, `experiment`, `code` and `dead-end` (displayed as Alternatives). The complete argument, narrative figures, comparison cases and verdict remain intelligible with every fold closed. Optional layers deepen that argument; they do not hide a separate required argument. A fold title is already its heading; do not repeat it inside. Preserve deep-link anchors when consolidating.

Choose by narrative weight, not element type: a table or equation may carry the Story; a plot may be supporting detail. Default to visible figures and folded reference density. Keep competing formulations together, with assumptions and verdict explicit. Add a new layer only for recurring content and follow the [maintenance procedure](../site/reader.md#extending-reading-layers).

When moving detail into a record, keep the former page anchor on the summary that replaces it and link to the matching record anchor. Keep concise lessons from rejected methods reachable, with verdict up front; detailed captures/reproduction remain evidence.

Naming and claims on a page follow the same rules as in its record: [naming and honest claims](../records.md#naming-and-honest-claims).
